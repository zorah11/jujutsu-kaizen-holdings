# Design direction

- Hero topology: full-bleed Ugandan highlands with a left-aligned editorial title and a slim vertical chapter marker.
- Navigation character: quiet ivory wordmark bar that becomes a compact forest header after scrolling.
- Typography pairing: Cormorant Garamond display with Manrope body and uppercase utility labels.
- Image treatment: photography behaves as architecture—full bleeds, offset crops, and framed windows rather than repeated cards.
- Section rhythm: alternate dark immersive scenes with spacious ivory editorial spreads and ruled index sections.
- Motion signature: restrained upward reveals and image-mask openings, disabled when reduced motion is preferred.

The supplied references contribute cinematic full-page pacing, oversized travel typography, translucent-but-readable overlays, and photographic itinerary storytelling. The final system avoids copying their interface chrome or unrelated medical/travel-booking details.
