About the company

Built with a wider view.

Jujutsu Kaizen Holdings Limited is a Ugandan company connecting a primary focus on travel and hospitality with broader interests in enterprise.

Who we are

A Ugandan company looking outward.

Based in Kira-Kitinda, Kira, Wakiso, we are positioned to explore opportunities in Uganda’s travel, tourism and hospitality landscape while maintaining the diversified character of a holdings company.

Our registered principal activity is travel agency and tour operator activities. That direction leads our public identity. The company’s permitted objects also create room to consider property-related activity, landscaping, general merchandise, procurement and other investments as they develop and are confirmed.

“Not one-sided” means keeping a clear focus while remaining ready for the connections that business creates.

Our business direction

Tourism first.
Possibility alongside it.

Travel and hospitality are the centre of our direction: bringing people closer to places, supporting enquiries and exploring opportunities across the visitor experience.

Alongside this, we maintain a professional interest in sectors that can support communities and commerce. We will describe active offerings only when they are verified and ready.

Our values

Clarity

We communicate what is known, what is developing and what still requires confirmation.

Professional service

We approach every conversation with preparation, courtesy and attention to the practical details.

Responsible ambition

We pursue opportunities with a long view—grounded in Uganda and aware of the wider world.

Our commitment

A credible first conversation.

Whether the subject is a future journey, hospitality, property or sourcing, our commitment begins with listening carefully and responding honestly.

We do not publish unverified claims, invented achievements or services that are not yet confirmed. This gives clients and collaborators a clear basis for deciding what comes next.
